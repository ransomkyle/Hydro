# Quick-start cards (print one per role, one page each)

## Everyone
1. Open the app on the tablet/phone. Sign in with **your own** username (never someone else’s).
2. Your **PIN** is your signature. Never share it. If you think someone knows it, tell QC now.
3. Every sign-off records your name and the exact time. You cannot edit one; QC can *revoke* with a reason.
4. The coloured dot top-right shows **Online / Offline**. Offline is fine — keep working; items sync by themselves. “⟳ n to sync” = waiting to send.

## Operator / Packer
- Open the shipment (scan the label QR with **📷 Scan** or search the job #).
- Work down the checklist: tap **Sign off…** → Pass / Fail / N/A → enter value or photo if asked → your PIN.
- **Fail** or **N/A** needs a short reason. A fail alerts QC automatically — keep going with other items.
- Log what you used on the **Materials** tab: heat/lot numbers (scan the tag with 📷), filler wire, gas, packaging.
- Photos: **Photos & docs** tab or inside the item.

## QC Inspector
- You sign items marked “QC inspector+”. **Hold points** (yellow tag) need a *different* QC person to **Verify**.
- Measurements: pick the **gauge** you used. An out-of-calibration gauge is blocked on purpose.
- Raise flags for anything wrong; resolve with a disposition (rework / repair / use-as-is / scrap) and your PIN.
- Open a **CAPA** from a flag when the cause needs fixing, not just the part.

## QC Manager
- Review → **Release for shipment…** (PIN). If items are open the screen lists them; releasing anyway needs a typed reason and alerts QC.
- **Checklists**: approve templates (PIN). Editing a template clears approval.
- **Reports** weekly; **CAPA** for overdue actions; **Gauges** for calibration due dates; **Audit → Verify chain** + record the **anchor** weekly.
- Reopen/cancel a shipment only with a reason; both are permanent in the audit trail.

## Shipping / Logistics
- Create shipments (or **Import CSV** from the ERP export). Fill carrier/driver/BOL/seal/permits on the **Logistics** tab.
- Print the **🏷 Label (QR)** — it says **HOLD — NOT RELEASED** until QC releases. Don’t load a HOLD label.
- Record **Ready / Loaded / Picked up / Delivered** events. For pickup get the driver’s name and signature on the screen and tap **Capture GPS**.
- Oversize? The tie-down guide on the Logistics tab shows the minimum count for the load length.

## Auditor (read-only)
- Everything is read-only. **Audit** page: filter, export CSV, **Verify chain**. Reports page for KPIs. Open any shipment → **Release package (PDF)**.
